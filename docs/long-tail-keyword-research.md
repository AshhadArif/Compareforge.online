**Date:** 2026-09-25
**Status:** Directional research for content planning. **No keyword volume, keyword difficulty, CPC or CPC-based revenue figures were collected** — no keyword tool with live data was accessed during this research cycle. ## 1. Method

- Opportunities are grouped by **intent** and mapped to an **existing target page** (all pages in the mapping below are live — nothing maps to a future page).
- Priority tiers are based on **first-party logic**: closeness to our shipped tools, uniqueness of our data/angle, and absence of a need for external data we cannot verify.
- Volume / KD / CPC columns intentionally left as `unavailable`. When a keyword tool is available later, fill the columns before re-ordering priorities — **do not guess them**.

Priority key: **T1** = closest to shipped tools, start here · **T2** = supported by existing data/guides · **T3** = topical coverage / supporting pages.
