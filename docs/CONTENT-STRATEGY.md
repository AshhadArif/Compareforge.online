# Content Strategy — CompareForge.online

## Strategy Overview

CompareForge is a **tool-first platform**: content exists to bring users into tools, explain results, and connect decisions — not to replace tools with static articles. Within that, the site still starts with a small number of carefully selected, high-quality pages in a focused category (smartphones) and expands only after the foundation is proven useful and technically sound.

**Principles:**
1. Tools are the product; content supports tools (`TOOL-CATEGORY-STRATEGY.md`).
2. Fewer high-quality pages > thousands of thin pages.
3. Every page has a clear intent and, where relevant, a registered tool behind its job.

## Initial Category Selection

### Selection Criteria

Before choosing the first category, evaluate against these criteria:

| Criterion | Weight | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| Search demand | High | Enough people search for comparisons in this category |
| Search intent | High | Searchers want comparison information, not just product pages |
| Competition | Medium | Existing comparisons are not so dominant that new content cannot compete |
| Commercial intent | Medium | Products have reasonable price points for AdSense/affiliate |
| Data availability | High | Reliable product specifications are accessible |
| Original value | High | Room to add analysis, explanation, and use-case guidance |
| Meaningful comparisons | High | Products have genuine differences worth comparing |
| Product lifecycle | Medium | Products are not replaced so quickly that content becomes outdated immediately |
| Update requirements | Low-Medium | Specifications don't change weekly |
| Legal/editorial risk | Low | No high-risk products (medical, financial, etc.) |
| Affiliate potential | Low | Retailers with affiliate programs exist |
| AdSense suitability | High | Category is appropriate for advertisers |
| Content depth | High | Enough material for thorough comparisons |

### Recommended Initial Category: **Smartphones**

**Why smartphones:**

1. **High search demand:** "iPhone vs Samsung," "Pixel vs iPhone," etc. are consistently searched
2. **Clear comparison intent:** Users actively compare specific models
3. **Data availability:** Specifications are publicly available from manufacturers
4. **Meaningful differences:** Smartphones have real, consequential differences in specs, features, and ecosystem
5. **Original value opportunity:** Specifications can be explained in plain language, use cases analyzed, trade-offs explored
6. **Product lifecycle:** Models release annually, giving comparisons a reasonable shelf life (12-18 months)
7. **Advertiser interest:** Smartphone comparisons attract relevant advertisers
8. **Comparison depth:** Rich enough for detailed, useful comparisons

**What to avoid in smartphone comparisons:**
- Comparing every possible model combination
- Creating pages for minor model variants
- Comparing phones at very different price points (unless intentional)
- Comparing phones from different generations (unless intentional)

**Initial smartphone comparisons (MVP):**
- iPhone 15 vs Samsung Galaxy S24
- iPhone 15 vs Google Pixel 8
- Samsung Galaxy S24 vs Google Pixel 8
- iPhone 15 Pro vs Samsung Galaxy S24 Ultra
- Budget smartphones comparison guide

## Content Pillars

### Pillar 0: Tool Support (primary)

Landing explanations, methodology notes, and FAQ content that make tool landings useful and indexable — each tied to a registry tool (`/tools/[tool]/`). Not keyword stubs; must pass the thin-page guard in `SEO-ARCHITECTURE.md`.

### Pillar 1: Product Comparisons

Direct head-to-head comparisons of two products (curated, editorial, demand-validated).

**Examples:**
- Product A vs Product B
- Category comparison pages

### Pillar 2: Feature Guides

Explanations of features, technologies, and specifications. Each guide should support a live tool or a genuine explanatory need.

**Examples:**
- What is OLED? (links to comparison tool / relevant curated compares)
- What is refresh rate and does it matter?
- Understanding processor benchmarks

### Pillar 3: Category Guides

Buying guides and overviews for product categories.

**Examples:**
- How to choose a smartphone
- Smartphone specifications explained
- What matters in a smartphone camera

### Pillar 4: Specification References

Detailed specification pages for individual products (optional, if value can be added).

**Examples:**
- iPhone 15 specifications (with analysis, not just a spec sheet)

## Content Calendar

### Phase 1 — Platform Foundation (tool-first)

**Content work:**
- Rewrite/repoint homepage + section intros to platform positioning (tools-first)
- Tool-support content for `product-comparison` and `product-finder` landings (how-it-works, methodology, FAQ)
- Keep/refresh curated comparisons and guides already built (re-link to tools)
- Ensure every guide names the tool it supports

**Explicitly not in this phase:** mass new comparison pages, new categories, tool-variant landing pages.

### Phase 2 — After compatibility + calculator tools ship

- Tool-support content for the two new landings
- Targeted curated pages/relations only where demand is validated (SERP format check)
- More guides only where they bridge to a live tool

### Phase 3 — Category expansion (gated)

- Category hub + guides for the new category
- Same tools, new category instance — content describes the category, not new tool names

**Standing rule:** volume follows data/editorial capacity and intent validation — never templates alone (Decisions 10/12).

## Content Rules

### Every Page Must

1. Have a clear purpose and search intent
2. Provide original analysis or value
3. Be evidence-based
4. Be reviewed for accuracy
5. Include source documentation
6. Be useful without affiliate links
7. Be accessible on mobile and desktop
8. Follow the editorial guidelines
9. Support a live registry tool or exist as genuine editorial (not a substitute for a missing tool)

### No Page Should

1. Exist solely for a keyword
2. Be thin or low-value
3. Copy content from other sources
4. Be auto-generated without review
5. Contain fake testing or experience claims
6. Contain unsupported claims
7. Be a duplicate or near-duplicate of another page
8. Contain placeholder or incomplete content
9. Duplicate a tool's job in bulk article form (SEO churn)
10. Be a doorway path to arbitrary dynamic tool states

## Content Quality Checklist

Before publishing any comparison page:

- [ ] Search intent is clear
- [ ] Introduction sets proper context
- [ ] Quick comparison provides immediate value
- [ ] Key differences are explained in plain language
- [ ] Detailed comparison adds original analysis
- [ ] Specification table is accurate and sourced
- [ ] Use-case analysis covers multiple scenarios
- [ ] Advantages/disadvantages are honest and balanced
- [ ] Sources are documented
- [ ] Related content is linked
- [ ] FAQ is genuinely useful (if included)
- [ ] No fake testing claims
- [ ] No keyword stuffing
- [ ] Mobile responsive
- [ ] All links work
- [ ] Grammar and spelling checked
- [ ] Facts verified

## Expansion Rules

### When to Add a New Comparison

- The comparison has meaningful search demand
- The two products have genuine differences worth comparing
- We can provide original value beyond what manufacturer pages offer
- The comparison fits within our current category focus
- We have reliable data sources for both products

### When NOT to Add a New Comparison

- The products are too similar to compare meaningfully
- The products are too different to compare (different categories)
- We cannot provide original value
- The comparison would be a near-duplicate of an existing page
- Product data is unreliable or unavailable
- The products are obsolete or very old

### When to Add a New Category

- The current category is well-established with sufficient content
- The new category meets all selection criteria
- We have the capacity to maintain content quality
- The new category has meaningful search demand
- The new category allows for original value creation

## Content Maintenance

### Regular Reviews

- **Monthly:** Check for outdated specifications
- **Quarterly:** Review content quality and accuracy
- **Biannually:** Assess category performance and expansion plans

### Update Triggers

- New product model released (comparison may need updating)
- Specification changes or corrections
- New information available
- User-reported errors
- Policy changes affecting content

### Retirement Criteria

- Products are no longer available or relevant
- Comparison has not received meaningful traffic in 12+ months
- Better comparison exists that supersedes this one
- Information is outdated and cannot be reasonably updated
