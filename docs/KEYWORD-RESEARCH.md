## 6. Content Brief Template

For each new comparison page:

### Structure
1. **H1:** [Phone A] vs [Phone B] Compared (Sep 2026)
2. **Quick Verdict:** 2-3 sentence summary with clear winner by use case
3. **Spec Comparison Table:** Side-by-side specs (display, chip, camera, battery, price)
4. **Design & Build:** Materials, dimensions, weight, water resistance
5. **Display:** Size, resolution, brightness, refresh rate
6. **Performance:** Chipset benchmarks, real-world speed
7. **Camera:** Day, night, video, zoom comparison
8. **Battery Life:** Screen-on time, charging speeds
9. **Software & AI:** OS features, AI capabilities, update commitment
10. **Key Differences:** Bullet-point summary
11. **Pros & Cons:** Each phone
12. **Who Should Buy Each:** Use-case recommendations
13. **FAQ:** 5-8 questions with structured data
14. **Related Comparisons:** Internal links

### SEO Requirements
- Title tag: "[Phone A] vs [Phone B] Compared (Sep 2026) | CompareForge"
- Meta description: 150-160 chars with comparison keywords
- H1 contains both phone names
- FAQPage JSON-LD structured data
- BreadcrumbList JSON-LD
- Internal links to related guides and comparisons
- Canonical URL set
- OG tags with comparison-specific image
