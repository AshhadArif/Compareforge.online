# Backlink Strategy — CompareForge.online

## Purpose

This document outlines a legitimate, sustainable backlink strategy for CompareForge. All strategies comply with Google's link spam policies. No paid links, link exchanges, or manipulative tactics.

---

## Strategy 1: Digital PR via Original Research

### Approach
Publish original research data that journalists and bloggers want to reference.

### Examples
- "How Smartphone Specifications Have Changed Over 5 Years" (aggregate data analysis)
- "Which Smartphone Brands Offer the Longest Software Support?" (data-driven comparison)
- "The Real Cost of Smartphone Ownership Over 3 Years" (total cost analysis)

### Execution
1. Compile data from public sources
2. Create a dedicated research page with charts and findings
3. Write a summary press release
4. Share with tech journalists and bloggers
5. Provide embeddable charts for easy attribution

### Target Publications
- Tech blogs and news sites
- Consumer advocacy publications
- Technology review sites

---

## Strategy 2: Guest Contributions

### Approach
Write genuinely useful articles for established technology publications.

### Guidelines
- Only contribute to publications that accept expert contributions
- Provide unique, original analysis — not rehashed content
- Include a natural, relevant link back to CompareForge where appropriate
- Focus on being helpful, not promotional

### Target Publications
- Medium technology publications
- Industry blogs
- Consumer advice sites

---

## Strategy 3: Resource Page Link Building

### Approach
Get CompareForge listed on resource pages that curate helpful comparison and research tools.

### Process
1. Identify resource pages (e.g., "Best Tech Research Tools," "Product Comparison Websites")
2. Verify the page is legitimate and editorially maintained
3. Reach out with a genuine pitch explaining why CompareForge adds value
4. Only pursue pages where a natural fit exists

### Qualification Criteria
- Page must be editorially curated (not a link farm)
- Page must have genuine traffic and authority
- Link must be editorial (not paid or exchanged)

---

## Strategy 4: Broken Link Building

### Approach
Find broken links on relevant pages and suggest CompareForge as a replacement.

### Process
1. Find broken links on comparison/research resource pages
2. Verify the broken link was previously relevant
3. Create content on CompareForge that matches the original intent
4. Reach out to the webmaster with the broken link and your replacement

### Rules
- Only suggest links where genuinely relevant
- Never create pages solely for broken link building
- Be helpful, not manipulative

---

## Strategy 5: Community Participation

### Approach
Participate genuinely in technology communities and forums.

### Platforms
- Reddit (r/smartphones, r/Android, r/apple, r/PickAnAndroidForMe)
- Quora (answer comparison questions)
- Technology forums
- Stack Exchange (where relevant)

### Rules
- Always provide genuine, helpful answers
- Only link to CompareForge when directly relevant
- Never spam or self-promote excessively
- Follow each platform's self-promotion guidelines
- Build reputation first, link second

---

## Strategy 6: HARO / Connectively / Quoted

### Approach
Respond to journalist queries about smartphone comparisons and technology.

### Process
1. Sign up for HARO (Help A Reporter Out) or similar services
2. Monitor queries related to smartphones, comparisons, consumer technology
3. Provide expert, helpful responses
4. If quoted, the journalist typically includes a link to your site

### Rules
- Only respond to queries where you can genuinely add value
- Be concise and helpful
- Never fabricate expertise

---

## Strategy 7: Content Partnerships

### Approach
Partner with complementary (non-competing) websites for mutual benefit.

### Examples
- Smartphone case manufacturers (comparison content + case recommendations)
- Phone repair services (specification data + repair cost comparisons)
- Mobile app review sites (hardware comparisons + software recommendations)

### Rules
- Partnership must be genuinely beneficial to users
- Links must be editorial, not exchanged
- Disclose any relationships

---

## Strategy 8: Unlinked Brand Mentions

### Approach
Find mentions of "CompareForge" or "compare forge" that don't link to the site.

### Process
1. Set up Google Alerts for "CompareForge" and variations
2. Monitor mentions using search tools
3. When an unlinked mention is found, reach out and request a link
4. Be polite and make it easy for the webmaster

---

## Link Building Rules

### Do
- Create genuinely useful content that deserves links
- Build relationships with journalists and bloggers
- Participate authentically in communities
- Provide expert analysis and original research
- Earn links through content quality

### Do NOT
- Buy links
- Exchange links reciprocally at scale
- Use private blog networks (PBNs)
- Create link schemes
- Use automated link building tools
- Participate in link wheels or link farms
- Submit to low-quality directories
- Use press releases solely for links
- Create content solely for link building

### Link Quality Criteria
- From a relevant, authoritative page
- Editorially placed (not paid or exchanged)
- Natural anchor text (not keyword-stuffed)
- On a page with genuine traffic
- From a site with real editorial standards

---

## Measuring Success

### Metrics
- Number of referring domains (quality over quantity)
- Domain authority growth over time
- Organic traffic growth
- Brand mention volume
- Referral traffic from earned links

### Timeline
- Month 1-3: Create foundational content, begin outreach
- Month 3-6: First links acquired, community presence established
- Month 6-12: Consistent link acquisition, authority growing
- Month 12+: Sustainable link growth from content quality

---

## Priority Actions

1. **Create 1-2 original research pieces** — most linkable assets
2. **Set up HARO alerts** — easiest way to earn editorial links
3. **Begin community participation** — builds reputation and natural links
4. **Resource page outreach** — targeted, high-quality link opportunities
5. **Broken link building** — high success rate when done correctly

---

## Notes

- Link building is a long-term strategy. Expect 6-12 months before significant results.
- Content quality is the foundation. Without great content, link building fails.
- Focus on earning links, not building them.
- One high-quality link from a relevant authority site is worth more than 100 low-quality links.
