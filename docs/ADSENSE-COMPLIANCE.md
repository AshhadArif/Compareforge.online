# Google AdSense Compliance — CompareForge.online

This document outlines requirements for Google AdSense readiness based on current official Google policies.

## Source of Requirements

Google AdSense program policies and Help Center documentation. This document should be updated when Google changes its policies.

Last reviewed: September 2026

---

## Content Requirements

### Original Content

**Requirement:** Content must be original and provide value to users.

**CompareForge approach:**
- All comparison analysis is original writing
- Specifications are sourced but analysis and interpretation are original
- No copied product descriptions from manufacturers or retailers
- No AI-generated bulk content published without review
- Feature explanations are written in plain language with original framing

**Rules:**
- Do NOT copy product descriptions from manufacturer websites
- Do NOT copy comparison text from other comparison sites
- Do NOT paraphrase manufacturer marketing as original content
- Do NOT publish AI-generated content without thorough review and editing
- Every comparison page must contain substantial original analysis

### Useful Content

**Requirement:** Content must be useful to users, not created primarily for search engine manipulation.

**CompareForge approach:**
- Every page answers a specific user question
- Comparisons help users understand real differences
- Specifications are explained, not just listed
- Use-case analysis helps users decide based on their needs
- No pages exist solely to capture a keyword

**Rules:**
- Do NOT create pages for keyword variations that add no value
- Do NOT create thin pages with minimal content
- Do NOT create pages that rephrase the same comparison differently
- Do NOT create doorway pages
- Every page must answer: "Why should a user read this instead of a manufacturer page?"

### Relevant Content

**Requirement:** Content must be relevant to the site's purpose.

**CompareForge approach:**
- All content relates to product comparison and research
- No random topics unrelated to the site's niche
- Categories are focused and logical

### Substantial Content

**Requirement:** Pages must contain enough content to be valuable.

**CompareForge approach:**
- Every comparison page has a minimum meaningful content threshold
- Pages are not padded with filler text
- Pages are not so thin they provide no real value
- Tables, analysis, and explanations provide substance

### Unique Comparisons

**Requirement:** Each comparison page must offer something different.

**CompareForge approach:**
- No duplicate comparison pages
- No pages targeting the same two products with minor wording changes
- Each comparison covers a distinct product pairing
- If two products appear in multiple contexts, consolidate rather than duplicate

## Navigation Requirements

### Clear Navigation

**Requirement:** Visitors must be able to find content easily.

**CompareForge approach:**
- Simple header navigation: Home, Comparisons, Categories, Guides, About
- Breadcrumbs on all pages
- Related content links on comparison and guide pages
- Search functionality
- Category pages for browsing
- No more than 3 clicks to reach any comparison from homepage

### No Deceptive Navigation

**Requirement:** Navigation must not mislead users.

**CompareForge approach:**
- No misleading buttons that look like system dialogs
- No fake "close" buttons on ads
- No deceptive interstitials
- No fake download buttons
- No misleading "next page" indicators
- Ads (when implemented) must be clearly distinguishable from content

### No Excessive Popups

**Requirement:** Popups and interstitials must not be excessive.

**CompareForge approach:**
- No popups on page load
- No interstitials blocking content
- No aggressive newsletter popups
- No deceptive overlays

### No Pages Under Construction

**Requirement:** All pages must be complete and functional.

**CompareForge approach:**
- No "coming soon" pages
- No placeholder content
- No empty category pages
- All links must work
- 404 page must be functional

## Content Risks to Avoid

### Scraped Content

**Risk:** Pulling product information from other sites without adding value.

**Prevention:**
- Never copy product descriptions wholesale
- Never copy comparison text from other sites
- Never scrape reviews or ratings from third-party sites
- Specifications may be referenced but must be clearly sourced and analyzed

### Copied Manufacturer Content

**Risk:** Using manufacturer marketing as site content.

**Prevention:**
- Never copy marketing copy from product pages
- Never use manufacturer bullet points as comparison content
- Never copy feature lists without analysis
- Always add original interpretation and context

### Thin Affiliate Pages

**Risk:** Creating pages with minimal content primarily to drive affiliate clicks.

**Prevention:**
- CompareForge does not have affiliate links in MVP
- When affiliate links are added, content must still provide substantial value
- No "buy now" pages with thin content
- Every page must work as a useful resource without affiliate links

### Automatically Generated Content

**Risk:** Using AI or scripts to mass-produce pages.

**Prevention:**
- No programmatic page generation from product databases without editorial review
- No AI-generated pages published without human review
- No template-driven pages with only product names swapped
- Every page must be reviewed for accuracy, originality, and usefulness

### Duplicate Content

**Risk:** Multiple pages targeting the same comparison.

**Prevention:**
- One page per product comparison
- No "Product A vs Product B" and "Product B vs Product A" as separate pages
- No similar pages with minor wording differences
- Canonical URLs properly implemented

### Keyword Stuffing

**Risk:** Over-optimizing page content with target keywords.

**Prevention:**
- Write naturally for users
- Do not repeat the comparison target unnaturally
- Do not stuff keywords in headings, alt text, or meta tags
- Focus on clarity, not keyword density

### Doorway Pages

**Risk:** Pages created solely to redirect or funnel users.

**Prevention:**
- Every page provides genuine content
- No pages that exist only to capture search traffic and redirect
- No thin pages that offer no value beyond a link to another page

### Misleading Claims

**Risk:** Making unsupported claims about products.

**Prevention:**
- Never claim a product is "the best" without evidence
- Never claim testing that did not occur
- Never fabricate benchmarks or results
- Always qualify claims with sources or evidence

### Fake Reviews and Ratings

**Risk:** Inventing product reviews or star ratings.

**CompareForge approach:**
- No fake star ratings
- No fabricated user reviews
- No invented review counts
- If displaying ratings, they must come from verifiable sources
- MVP: no ratings at all to avoid this risk

### Fake Testimonials

**Risk:** Inventing user testimonials.

**Prevention:**
- No fake testimonials on any page
- No invented user quotes
- No fabricated success stories

## Privacy Requirements

### Privacy Policy

**Requirement:** A clear privacy policy must be accessible.

**Requirements for AdSense:**
- Must disclose Google advertising cookies
- Must explain how user data is collected and used
- Must comply with applicable privacy laws
- Must be linked from every page (footer)

### Cookie Disclosure

**Requirement:** Users must be informed about cookies.

**When AdSense is implemented:**
- Disclose Google AdSense cookies
- Disclose Google Analytics cookies (if used)
- Provide cookie management options where required
- Link to cookie policy

### Contact Forms

**Requirement:** If contact forms collect data, privacy implications must be disclosed.

**CompareForge approach:**
- Minimal data collection
- Privacy policy explains what data contact forms collect
- No unnecessary data collection

### Affiliate Links

**Requirement:** Affiliate relationships must be disclosed.

**CompareForge approach (future):**
- Clear disclosure on pages with affiliate links
- Disclosure in disclaimer page
- Affiliate links clearly identified
- Editorial independence maintained

### Third-Party Services

**Requirement:** Third-party services must be disclosed.

**CompareForge approach:**
- Privacy policy lists all third-party services
- Analytics service disclosed
- Advertising service disclosed (when implemented)
- CDN or hosting provider disclosed if relevant

**Checklist:**

**CompareForge approach:**

- At least one guide

**Checklist:**

- No violent content
- No illegal content
- No hate speech
- No misleading content
- No malware
- No phishing
- No deceptive practices

## Application Timing

**Requirement:** Apply when the site is ready for review.

**Criteria for application:**
- Site is live and accessible
- Content is complete and high-quality
- Navigation works properly
- Privacy policy is in place
- No obvious policy issues
- Site provides genuine value to users
- Sufficient content for review (quality, not quantity)

**Do NOT apply:**
- Before the site has meaningful content
- When the site has obvious issues
- When content is clearly AI-generated without review
- When the site looks like a thin affiliate site

## Tool Platform Requirements (multi-tool site)

As CompareForge becomes a multi-tool platform, the following additional rules apply.

### Tool landing pages

**Risk:** scaled/thin pages if each tool (or tool variant) gets a doorway-style landing.

**Rules:**
- Each `/tools/[tool]/` landing must independently help a user: what it does, who it's for, how it works, methodology/sources, FAQ, related tools/guides — useful even before running the tool
- One landing per registered tool `purpose`; **no duplicate-purpose variants** (`x-tool` vs `x-calculator` vs `x-checker` doing the same job)
- Tool landings are in the sitemap only when `status: built` in the registry; planned tools are not indexable stubs
- Titles/H1s describe the actual job, not keyword permutations

### Dynamic tool states

- All arbitrary tool outputs (selector states, wizard results, calculator states) are **`noindex`**
- Never submitted to the sitemap
- Not used to capture long-tail permutations at scale (this would be scaled content abuse / doorway behavior)

### Tool output honesty

- Results must not fabricate testing, ratings, win-rate scores, or "lab verified" claims (aligns with Decisions 2/3)
- Calculations must disclose assumptions and dates; no pretending to give financial, medical, or legal advice
- Compatibility verdicts: "Not verified" when data missing — never a fabricated Yes/No
- Finder/recommendation outputs: explain *why*; never a single universal "best" presented as fact without criteria

### Tools vs content

- Do not publish static articles that merely rephrase what a tool already does, in bulk, for keyword coverage
- Do not publish articles *instead of* building a registered tool solely to capture its queries if the right answer is interactive (quality/user-value alignment)
- Guides must support a live tool or genuine explanatory need; no orphan SEO pages

### Inventory of tool pages at review time

Maintain an internal list: registry `built` count = number of indexable tool landings. Mismatch = fix before AdSense review or after approval.

## Ongoing Compliance

After AdSense approval, maintain compliance by:

- Regularly reviewing Google AdSense policies for updates
- Ensuring new content meets quality standards
- Monitoring ad placement for policy compliance
- Responding to policy violation notifications promptly
- Maintaining privacy policy accuracy
- Keeping content original and useful
- Not sacrificing content quality for ad revenue
- Re-running the tool-landing inventory check whenever a tool is added or retired
